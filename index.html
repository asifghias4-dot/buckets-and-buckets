About Us

We opened our doors on March 17, 2020, only to be shut down the same evening due to Covid-related closures. That did not stop us, it only motivated us to work harder.

Through hard work, sweat and tears, we now serve an ever-improving menu. A big part of it is based on your feedback and suggestions, and that will never change.

Good to know:

  • Food from other vendors is not allowed on the premises (except baby food).
  • We are a boycotting restaurant and do not serve or allow boycott-list products.
  • Apron sets are handed out based on bucket size. Extra apron sets are OMR 0.250.

We appreciate your cooperation.
